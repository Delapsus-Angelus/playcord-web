How to add me

  1. Get PlayCord. It’s being tested on iPhone just now: ask me for an invite.
  2. Tap Open in PlayCord above: Friends opens with my code in it. Tap Add.
  3. Or open PlayCord yourself, tap Friends in the bar, type or paste the code under “Got a friend’s code?”, and tap Add.
  4. We’re friends both ways, and you can see what we share.

No Friends in PlayCord?

Your PlayCord is on an older version. If it says “PlayCord is ready. Restart to use it”, tap Restart. If not, close PlayCord fully (swipe it away) and open it again: the new version is used from then on.
