6. Membership and AI credit

A membership and AI credit are bought in the app through Apple’s App Store, under Apple’s own terms as well as these. Apple takes the payment; we never see your card details.

A membership is a subscription, monthly or yearly, at the price the App Store shows before you confirm. It renews automatically for the same length unless you cancel it at least 24 hours before the end of the current period, and Apple charges your Apple ID for the renewal within the 24 hours before that. Manage or cancel it in your App Store account settings (Settings, your name, Subscriptions). Cancelled, it runs to the end of the period you’ve paid for.

Membership’s price at launch is PlayCord’s launch price, £3.99 a month or £24.99 a year, until the end date shown in the app. Members who join by then keep that price for as long as their membership carries on; under Apple’s rules, a membership that lapses can be taken up again at that price within 60 days. While the launch lasts, members get 50p of AI credit a month; when it ends, the monthly AI credit ends for everyone, members who joined in the launch included. After the launch, membership for new members is £5.99 a month or £37.99 a year. We may change the price for new members after that; for an existing member who didn’t join in the launch, Apple tells you before a new price applies to your renewals, and you can cancel before then.

AI credit given free is a launch offer, while the launch lasts: the welcome credit for an account made before it ends, and members’ monthly AI credit until it ends. Credit already given stays yours to use.

AI credit is bought in packs and kept until it’s used; it has no value outside PlayCord and can’t be exchanged for money. Credit packs aren’t refundable by us. Refunds for anything bought in the app, membership or credit, are made by Apple, at reportaproblem.apple.com, under Apple’s rules. If Apple refunds a credit pack, the part of its credit you haven’t used is taken back; if Apple refunds a membership, it ends.

A tip, from the tip jar, is a thank-you to PlayCord: it doesn’t add credit, a membership or anything else to your account. Refunds of tips are Apple’s, as above.

If you delete your account, a membership bought through Apple carries on renewing until you cancel it in your App Store account settings, and unused credit is lost.
