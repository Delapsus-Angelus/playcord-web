Reports and complaints

What happens when you report something in PlayCord, and how to complain. Last updated 9 November 2026.

If someone is in danger right now, call 999. To report images of child sexual abuse anywhere online, use the Internet Watch Foundation, report.iwf.org.uk.

Reporting something

Every post, comment, message and profile in PlayCord has Report. You can also block anyone you meet, friend or not. Or write to support@playcord.app, saying what you saw and where.

What happens next

We look at every report within three working days. Anything illegal or against our terms is taken down as soon as we find it. We may hide or remove it, and suspend or close the account that posted it.

A post on a message board that two members report is hidden until we’ve looked at it.

If we’ve acted on something of yours

If we’ve removed something you wrote, or suspended or closed your account, and you think we got it wrong, write to support@playcord.app with what it was and why. We’ll look again and reply within ten working days. If we were wrong, we’ll put it right.

Complaints

To complain about how we handled a report, about something we haven’t taken down, or about safety in PlayCord, write to support@playcord.app. We reply within ten working days, saying what we’ve done.

Questions about any of this: support@playcord.app
