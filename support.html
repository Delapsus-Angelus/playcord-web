Support

Help with PlayCord, the app that turns your playlists into a record collection.

Write to us: support@playcord.app. Say which email you sign in with, and what you were doing when it went wrong. A screenshot helps. You can also use Report a problem in the app.

Signing in

PlayCord signs you in with a code sent to your email, with no password to remember. If it hasn’t arrived after a minute, check your junk or spam folder. You can ask for a new code after 20 seconds.

Membership and AI credit

Membership and AI credit are bought in the app, through the App Store. To see them, open PlayCord’s Settings, then Membership and AI. Restore purchases there adds anything this Apple ID has bought that isn’t showing.

To cancel a membership, open your iPhone’s Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions. It stays on until the end of the period you’ve paid for.

Apple takes the payment, so refunds are Apple’s to give: ask at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Your data

To download everything PlayCord keeps about you, open Settings, then You, then Download my data.

To delete your account and everything in it, open Settings, then You, then Delete my account. It can’t be undone, and a membership bought through Apple carries on until you cancel it as above.

The privacy policy says what’s kept and why. To ask about your data, write to support@playcord.app.

Something that worries you

Every post, comment, message and profile in PlayCord has Report, and you can block anyone you meet. Or write to support@playcord.app. Reports and complaints says what happens next.

Questions about any of this: support@playcord.app
