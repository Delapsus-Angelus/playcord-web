PlayCord

Your music, on vinyl

PlayCord reads the songs you play and works out which records are worth owning, in what order, within your monthly budget.

Coming soon to the App Store

What it does

  • Finds the albums behind the songs you love, and ranks them for your shelf.
  • Plans what to buy each month, within the budget you set.
  • Checks UK record shops for where each one is cheapest, delivery included.
  • Keeps your collection, your scores and your all-time greats in one place, to share with friends.
