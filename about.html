Album covers

Album covers come from the Cover Art Archive (coverartarchive.org, run by MetaBrainz and the Internet Archive), found by each album’s MusicBrainz entry. On an album’s page, the artwork is Apple Music’s where Apple has the album, shown with a link to it on Apple Music.

Covers and artwork belong to their copyright owners, usually the record labels and the artists. PlayCord shows them so you can recognise each album, and doesn’t claim any right in them.

If you own the rights to a cover and want it taken out of PlayCord, write to support@playcord.app and it will be removed.

Where the information comes from

Which album each song is on, each album’s details, track list and pressings, the year it came out, the albums found when you search, the albums the AI picks (each checked there), and which release a barcode is, come from MusicBrainz (musicbrainz.org), the open music database, whose core data is free for anyone to use.

Exchange rates: the European Central Bank.

Record shops: data from Overture Maps Foundation (overturemaps.org), from Meta, Microsoft and others under CDLA Permissive 2.0, and Foursquare (© 2024 Foursquare Labs, Inc., Apache 2.0), each shop then checked by PlayCord’s AI. Where a town or postcode is: postcodes.io, containing OS data © Crown copyright and database right, Royal Mail data © Royal Mail copyright and database right, and ONS data © Crown copyright and database right.

Some answers come from Anthropic’s Claude AI: which album a song belongs to, shop prices found on the web, what a record shop’s own site shows for a record, suggestions, an artist’s best-known albums, the best albums in a genre, the equipment in a photo, what a receipt, an order or a shop’s page says, and the story of how an album was made. They can be wrong.

An album’s story is told from its Wikipedia article, by the article’s contributors, and shared as the article is, under CC BY-SA 4.0: each story names and links its article.

Shop names, Apple, Apple Music, Spotify and other names in PlayCord are trade marks of their owners. PlayCord isn’t connected with them, and they don’t endorse it.

Prices and buying

Prices, stock and delivery are as found on each shop’s own website when they were checked, with the date. They can change at any time, so check with the shop before you buy. Where a price is an estimate, PlayCord says so.

You buy from the shop, not from PlayCord. PlayCord doesn’t sell records and isn’t part of your purchase.

Questions about any of this: support@playcord.app
